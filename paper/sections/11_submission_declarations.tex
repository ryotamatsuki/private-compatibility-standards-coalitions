\section*{Computational verification and AI-assisted research methods}
ChatGPT (OpenAI) was used as an AI-assisted research tool during selected stages of the research process, including theoretical and alternative-case exploration, algebraic checking, literature and source discovery, and the development, editing, and review of research code and reproducibility scripts. AI-generated output was not treated as a mathematical proof or as an authoritative scholarly source. The author retained final scientific control over the research question, current model specification and assumptions, theorem statements, the acceptance or rejection of theoretical claims, economic interpretation, scope, and final claims. Product-market expressions and welfare comparisons were analytically derived or re-derived; independently executable SymPy scripts computationally cross-check stated algebraic identities, while numerical scripts test behavior over the stated admissible domain without being treated as proof. Bibliographic claims were checked against original articles, publisher records, DOI records, or other authoritative sources. The verification scripts and outputs are included in the accompanying anonymous replication package.
