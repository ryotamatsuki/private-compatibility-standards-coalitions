\section{Explicit Coalition-Deviation and Payoff Ledger}\label{app:short-coalition-ledger}
This supplementary appendix closes the finite coalition-stability proof in
Section 5 of the short main article. It retains the five formal partitions and
all admissible deviation coalitions, rather than relying on source code as a
substitute for a game-theoretic argument. The formal deviation map is
$\rho'(\rho,\mathcal D)=\{\mathcal D\}\cup
\{B\setminus\mathcal D:B\in\rho,B\setminus\mathcal D\ne\varnothing\}$.
All firms optimize again after a change of partition; no transfers are available.

\subsection{Complete enumeration by symmetry}
With $N=\{1,2,3\}$, the seven possible nonempty deviation coalitions are
$\{1\},\{2\},\{3\},\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{2,3\},N$. The table below
shows the induced outcomes for a representative $SU_{12}$; all other unions
follow by relabeling. A dash indicates the same original partition.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}\toprule
From & Deviators $\mathcal D$ & Induced partition \\
\midrule
$SW$ & any singleton; any pair; $N$ & $SW$; $SU_{ij}$; $IS$\\
$IS$ & any singleton; any pair; $N$ & $SU_{jk}$; $SU_{ij}$; $IS$\\
$SU_{12}$ & $\{1\},\{2\}$ & $SW$\\
$SU_{12}$ & $\{3\},\{1,2\}$ & $SU_{12}$ (no change)\\
$SU_{12}$ & $\{1,3\}$ & $SU_{13}$\\
$SU_{12}$ & $\{2,3\}$ & $SU_{23}$\\

\bottomrule\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here $\{i,j\}$ denotes any pair and $k$ its complement. Singleton
breakaways from $IS$ produce a regional union of the two countries remaining
together (the singleton deviator becomes its outsider).

\subsection{Continuation welfare by regime}
For the symmetric benchmark let $H=(1+2c)^2/16$,
$S=(1-2c)^2/16$, and $K_W=(3-2c)^2/32$. The government-welfare
rows are
\begin{align*}
W^{SW}&=K_W+H+2S,&W^{IS}&=K_I+3P,\\
W_M^{SU,N}&=K_M+2A+C,&W_O^{SU,N}&=K_O+2B+D,\\
W_M^{SU,O}&=K_I+2P+C,&W_O^{SU,O}&=K_O+2P+D-F.
\end{align*}
The following signs are exact throughout the canonical admissible domain
where explicitly invoked: $W^{IS}>W^{SW}$ and
$W_M^{SU,N}>W^{SW}$ (Appendix B). On $\Omega_0$ additionally
$W_M^{SU,N}>W^{IS}>W_O^{SU,N}$ and $P-C>0$.
The last inequality with $J=K_I+P-K_O-D>0$ gives
\[
W^{IS}-W_M^{SU,O}=P-C>0,\quad
W^{IS}-W_O^{SU,O}=J+F>0.
\]
These exact comparisons, rather than the calculation of new numerical
examples, determine each deviation sign.

\subsection{Five-partition proof under strict and weak blocking}
\emph{High $F>2T_A$:} Every $SW$ is blocked by the grand coalition $N$
forming $IS$. Every $IS$ is blocked by a two-country coalition forming
$SU_{ij}$, since both its members strictly gain. For a regional $SU_{ij}$:
(i) each member's solo breakaway produces $SW$ and reduces welfare;
(ii) an outsider-only deviation and the incumbent members' re-forming the
same pair leave the partition unchanged;
(iii) switching partner from $SU_{ij}$ to $SU_{ik}$ makes $i$ indifferent
by symmetry, so it fails the strict-blocking test;
(iv) the grand coalition would make each incumbent member worse off
because $W_M^{SU,N}>W^{IS}$. These cases exhaust the seven deviating
coalitions, and all three SUs are strictly unblocked. For weak/Pareto
blocking, in a switch from $SU_{12}$ to $SU_{13}$, country 1 is indifferent
and country 3 strictly improves from outsider to member because
$W_M^{SU,N}>W^{IS}>W_O^{SU,N}$. This weakly blocks every SU, so the weak
stable set is empty at exact symmetry.

\emph{Intermediate $F_L<F<2T_A$:} Every $SW$ is blocked by $N$ forming
$IS$. Every $SU$ is blocked by $N$ forming $IS$ because the two members
gain $P-C>0$ and the outsider gains $J+F>0$. From $IS$ each proper
nontrivial coalition creates some $SU$, but all deviators lose in its
continuation; $\mathcal D=N$ reproduces $IS$. Thus $IS$ is uniquely
unblocked under either strict or weak/Pareto blocking. No sufficiency or
necessity is inferred outside these fixed-cost regimes.

\subsection{Small asymmetry}
For $m_1=m_2=1$ and $m_3=1-\delta$, re-solve the private-adoption games
for each induced partition. At high $F$ the partner-switching payoff
difference is exactly
$W_1^{12,N}-W_1^{13,N}=\delta(A-C)>0$.
For sufficiently small $\delta>0$, strict inequalities in all other
cases persist by continuity, giving unique $SU_{12}$ under both concepts.
At intermediate $F$, for member $i$ and outsider $o$, the relevant
signs are $W_i^{IS}-W_i^{SU,O}=m_o(P-C)>0$ and
$W_o^{IS}-W_o^{SU,O}=m_oJ+F>0$. The original R6 exhaustive check certifies
these conclusions for its stated exact witness ranges; we make no claim of
new bounds or of endogenous membership renegotiation.
