## Why JICT is first

JICT's current statement — “Applied Theory. Innovative Empirical Analysis. Economic Policy” — matches the final architecture better than IJIO's higher general-insight bar. The journal describes its scope as the microeconomic foundation of markets for goods/services, firm strategy, competition, innovation/new technologies, and international trade, and explicitly welcomes applied theoretical papers with empirical/policy implications.

The paper can therefore be submitted on its actual strength:

1. exact conditional government-incentive mechanism in a transparent microfoundation;
2. non-knife-edge incomplete-adaptation open set;
3. falsification/scope tests rather than unsupported portability claims;
4. institutional stability application.

No claim upgrade is needed for fit.

## Policy compatibility

Springer Nature's updated September 2026 AI framework treats AI use by risk and explicitly permits, with disclosure and human oversight, uses including drafting sections based on author input, restructuring, explanatory summaries, modelling suggestions and analytical assistance. Red-line uses are opaque/substitutive uses where AI replaces accountable scholarly judgment.

The repository records human accountability, clean-room algebra, independent symbolic/numerical verification, Lean checks, source verification, and explicit AI disclosure. On that documented basis the project is compatible with the current Springer Nature framework, provided the disclosure remains complete.
