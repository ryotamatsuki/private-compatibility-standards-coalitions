## K0: Exact sunk-payment nonresult (simple Stage 2 kill calculation, NOT a novel theorem)

Fix investment/adoption state a selected before the government's renegotiation move, and the same admissible sunk payment K_i(a) in each counterfactual new formal partition rho. Let government's continuation welfare from the state be

  W_i(rho; a) = V_i(rho; a) - K_i(a).

For any rho, rho', by cancellation,

  W_i(rho;a) - W_i(rho';a) = V_i(rho;a) - V_i(rho';a).

SymPy independent symbolic smoke check of legacy-style normalization:
 W_SU = E-D-F; W_IS=-F; simplify(W_SU-W_IS) = E-D;
 W_SU_post = -D+R-F; W_IS_post=-F; simplify(W_SU_post-W_IS_post)=-D+R;
 derivative with respect to F of government policy difference = 0.

**Scope caution:** K0 assumes the *same fixed installed asset and same sunk payment* in both government renegotiation alternatives. It does NOT apply to ex ante comparison of different histories/adoption profiles, to deployment or abandonment contingent on rho, or to state-dependent switching/investment payoffs. Thus merely making the original F sunk without generating a regime-specific state-dependent product-market continuation cannot change ex post government regime rankings. Claiming this elementary cancellation as publication-level novelty would fail.
