## Conflict hard gate before any names are entered

For each selected reviewer, the author must confirm:

- not at the same institution;
- no recent coauthorship;
- no advisor/advisee relationship;
- no active joint project;
- no close personal/family relationship;
- no other relationship that could reasonably impair independence.

Do not exclude a scholar merely because they might be demanding or because they authored close prior work.
