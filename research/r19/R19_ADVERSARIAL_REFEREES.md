## Referee 1 — Industrial organization

**Major concern.** A correct new comparative static of residual import cost does not prove the requested economic origin of outsider-only investment. In `G_o(d)=2[ell*y(d)^2-pi_o^B(c)]`, the B-state term is a separately determined equilibrium object. Its derivatives are not being characterized when d varies. This is a post-investment comparative static holding the network switch fixed, not the full private compatibility decision. The result is interesting precisely because it refutes monotone alignment of private gains and the member's institutional preference; it cannot simultaneously be sold as proof that a stronger private gain induces a stronger reversal.

**Minor concern.** The factor two uses two destinations with the same three suppliers and symmetric national ownership. Increasing destinations while keeping firms fixed differs from increasing coalition member countries, which generally changes the number of suppliers. A market-count theorem based on replicating destinations must say which experiment it describes.

**Mathematical counterexample/boundary.** The R9 homogeneous Cournot residual derivative is positive for all `v<1/4`; the differentiated Bertrand derivative at zero is negative for the entire `0<=v<=gamma<1` substitute class. Hence the claimed same-sign common residual incidence lemma is false. Conversely, the opposite signs do not preclude the common Level-2 ranking theorem, which accommodates either r sign.

**Unproved lemma.** Primitive inequalities ranking every SU reverse investment and every SW standard increment below the outsider's two-market gain, uniformly over all rival portfolios.

**Overlap.** Costly one-way compatibility and firm-stage games are prior in Buccella–Fanti–Gori and Manenti–Somma. Fixed setup economies of scope are elementary. The new residual comparative static may be an applied national-incidence result.

**Minimum acceptance condition.** Present the partial theorem as such. Either derive joint adoption/policy restrictions from clear primitives or preserve the current narrow contribution without claiming Level 3.

## Referee 2 — Microeconomic theory

**Major concern.** The general coefficient ell is a derived market-conduct object. Calling a condition in ell “primitive” without separately deriving ell from actual strategies would recreate R18's problem. Here that derivation is available for the specified linear Cournot and linear Bertrand games, so the two subtheorems are primitive within those classes. They do not establish a primitive arbitrary-conduct theorem.

**Minor concern.** `gamma=1` makes M0 singular, so the R9 nesting must retain R13's tied homogeneous buyers and quantity-clearing allocation. It is not a strictly concave differentiated representative-consumer case. Consumer welfare must remain `q'M0q/2`, never `q'(M0-vC)q/2`.

**Mathematical counterexample/boundary.** Strictly concave utility, positive inverse-demand cross effects, and a positive definite/diagonally dominant Cournot Jacobian do not guarantee every rival quantity falls when one firm's cost falls. The parent's exact three-brand counterexample establishes this. It is a counterexample to inverse-demand substitution plus curvature, not to full Marshallian gross substitutes.

**Unproved lemma.** Demand and network cross-partial restrictions that uniformly deliver the national-incidence magnitude needed for the initial SU ranking, without assuming that ranking or an equivalent endogenous bound.

**Overlap.** Consumer envelope and markups/pass-through incidence are established; no independent priority claim follows from their exact specialization.

**Minimum acceptance condition.** Separate consumer primitives, technology primitives, conduct derivations, equilibrium response conditions and numerical witness checks in every theorem statement.

## Referee 3 — Mathematical economics

**Major concern.** Fully compatible M being positive definite does not establish the full investment game. At `gamma=9/10,v=4/5`, the full-compatible determinant is `1/250>0`, but the 2+1 network matrix determinant is `-33/250`. Hence the full-compatible residual theorem cannot certify other subgames' existence, uniqueness or demand consistency.

**Minor concern.** A positive rho gives an unconstrained minimum d_min, but that minimum may lie beyond the economic interval. The technologically relevant range is the intersection of positive outputs, `d<=c`, strict investment selection and any global-game conditions. The U-shape must be truncated accordingly.

**Mathematical counterexample/boundary.** See the exact determinant example. Also one may not infer global price Nash from interior FOCs. The newly supplied equicorrelation proof does cover the fully compatible family globally, including entrant-at-choke kinks; the numerical quartic Bertrand roots do not obtain that proof automatically.

**Unproved lemma.** General nonlinear Bertrand global equilibrium uniqueness under C3 perturbations, including bounded strategy compactification and every corner/kink. Existing rational witness global tests are different evidence.

**Overlap.** Strong monotonicity, Rosen-type uniqueness, implicit functions and inverse-matrix formulas are tools, not new economics.

**Minimum acceptance condition.** Keep finite rational global-Nash/entry certificates and the new full-compatible analytical proof. Label nonlinear tests numerical and arbitrary-conduct continuation conditional.

## Referee 4 — Trade and standards policy

**Major concern.** The residual boundary `R(d)=D` is conditional on a separately established export disadvantage D. No sign theorem about d proves D>0 or the initial regional benefit. The institutional mechanism still needs the persistent directional market-access asymmetry; a comparative static of national welfare alone is not a theory of standards agreements.

**Minor concern.** Changing a government's counterfactual SU/IS ranking is not an actual renegotiation equilibrium. Formal treaty status remains fixed throughout private adaptation. Coalition stability is only inherited within the original proved scope.

**Mathematical counterexample/boundary.** For the original zero-network Cournot case, the initial SU-minus-IS gap is `-47/3200`, so outsider gains or private scope by themselves cannot supply the initial protection advantage. Removing the outbound disadvantage can destroy the complete-bypass IS superiority, even though the outsider still has a private investment incentive.

**Unproved lemma.** A primitive common cause that jointly delivers initial protection net of outbound loss, strictly selected one-way investment, and the post-adoption sign.

**Overlap.** Gandal–Shy already has regional standards and price competition; Maggi–Mrázová already has public standard choice followed by firms' fixed-cost adaptation; Walz–Woeckener already has regional standardization and third-market competition. Latest/full-source gaps remain material.

**Minimum acceptance condition.** State the narrow residual candidate, show theorem-level differences from accessible nearest texts, and do not claim priority against uninspected 2026 or CEPR proofs.
