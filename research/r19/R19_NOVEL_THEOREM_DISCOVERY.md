## Strongest new sharp primitive boundary

For differentiated Cournot with `0<=v<1/4` and `v<=gamma<=1`,

`gamma_C*(v)=[2v-1+sqrt(17+20v-28v^2)]/4`.

The initial residual-cost component decreases with d below this product
substitutability cutoff, is flat to first order at equality, and increases
above it. The proof is the strictly increasing gamma-polynomial in the
T-R source. The original homogeneous gamma=1 is above the cutoff. In
substitute differentiated Bertrand, rho>0 throughout `v<=gamma<1`.
Hence the economic cause is quantitative substitution plus markups, not
simply a universal distinction between price and quantity competition.

A same-gamma,v PARTIAL contrast is valid at the R16 gamma,v pair:
Cournot `rho=-19424/63375<0`, Bertrand `rho=222258/276875>0`.
This says nothing about a shared full-mechanism witness: the old R10
same-parameter complete-reversal assertion remains withdrawn.

## Counterfactual tests required by Stage 9

1. **Exogenize investment.** T-R still holds as a cost-incidence theorem;
   endogenous adoption selection and cutoff d_A no longer follow. Thus
   T-R alone cannot be sold as the new whole private-government mechanism.
2. **Remove access aggregation.** The factor-two technology disappears.
   At both positive witnesses the outsider's per-destination gain is
   below the largest reverse/SW gain, while the aggregate exceeds it.
   This is a real selection role, but scope itself is known. This test
   does not prove aggregation necessary in every conceivable economy.
3. **Remove the outbound handicap.** At full bypass d=0 the member
   post-gap becomes zero rather than negative. In R9, `R(d)>=0` on the
   original domain, so setting D=0 also removes strict post-IS preference.
   In the affine Bertrand residual component, `R(d)<0` for sufficiently
   small d>0, so a negative post component can persist algebraically even
   at D=0. This is a counterfactual accounting observation, not a certified
   full investment equilibrium of a separately redesigned export market.
4. **Weaken networks.** Exact `v=0,c=.1,d=.05,F=.02` retains unique
   one-way adoption and SW no support but has negative initial SU gap.
   Networks are not necessary for adoption; they are important to initial
   SU advantage in this maintained R9 family. No universal necessity for
   the broad selective-erosion idea is asserted.
5. **Change conduct.** The common T-R algebra survives with separately
   derived ell; the residual slope changes across a primitive cutoff.
   Original R9 and R16 complete positive examples both re-execute, using
   their distinct primitives. Arbitrary nonlinear price games remain open.
6. **Combine existing general theory.** Finite dominance, scope,
   inverse-Jacobian incidence and continuity produce many components.
   T-R is an exact affine specialization, not new general mathematics.
   The full common-cause primitive conjunction has not been derived, and
   the latest close texts remain unavailable; no independent Level 4 claim.

## Stopping decision

Do not add more demand examples to substitute for missing primitive
lemmas. The useful bounded partial theorems and new kink proof are saved.
Preserve R18 as a conditional theory and prioritize a correctly scoped
submission draft over another unrestricted generalization round. Further
research is justified only by a concrete primitive adoption/incidence
inequality or new source access, not by model proliferation.
