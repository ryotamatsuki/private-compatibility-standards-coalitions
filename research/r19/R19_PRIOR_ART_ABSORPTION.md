# R19 — Prior-art absorption and source-access audit

Date: 2026-10-20. Research-only, baseline R18 `6cd349daec244def5cb4ed475cc448ed091b9c22`. No inherited file is amended. Independent literature-role audit; this is not an external human referee report.

## 1. Decision and reading standard

**No Level-4 priority certification.** The institutional-feedback conjunction remains a potentially distinctive applied result, but neither failure to locate an identical proposition nor a more general Jacobian calculation proves independent novelty. The latest Maggi–Mrázová revision, the 2026 Buccella–Fanti–Gori article, and Walz–Woeckener's DP3816 remain material full-proof gaps.

The classifications below distinguish *availability of the entire primary document* from *what this R19 audit actually inspected*. `P` means relevant model, proposition text and indicated proof inspected in a full primary-text resource. It does **not** assert that every line of every appendix was re-proved. `A` means only authoritative abstract/record or publisher excerpts. `U` means the relevant full theorem text is unavailable. An older working paper is not certified identical to the final publication. Historical R17/R18 readings are identified separately and are not silently converted into new R19 readings.

No secondary-only source is used to certify a theorem's absence. Searches extended to recent endogenous market access, trade-agreement erosion and public/private interoperability. They did not yield a newly verified proposition that resolves the identified priority gaps; this is a limited search result, not an exhaustive literature theorem.

## 3. Explicit maximum-absorption test

For the purpose of screening, credit **all** of the following to earlier work: unilateral compatibility; private endogenous investment; fixed costs and scope; public standards followed by adaptation; regional public recognition; world versus regional welfare; both price and quantity conduct; national incidence; bloc-size access aggregation; and private/public technological substitution or complementarity.

Logical correspondence of the non-novel components:

1. `F < gross profit improvement` and all-profile dominance are finite-game payoff comparisons. The market-count factor follows from a common interface and separable destinations. Combining them does not invent a new equilibrium concept.
2. Re-solving a regular market game and applying `x_z = -Phi_x^{-1} Phi_z` is the implicit-function theorem. Adding standard quasi-linear surplus differentials gives incidence calculations already available in general form.
3. Integrating the incidence identities to compare policy endpoints is calculus. Bounding an integral by the lower/upper envelope cannot acquire economic novelty by calling the constants primitive.
4. Continuity of strict adoption/ranking inequalities around an exact witness establishes an open neighborhood, but is a standard robustness argument.

What is **not yet logically obtained from those tools alone** is a substantive primitive restriction forcing the *joint* sequence: outsider support is strictly privately optimal, reciprocal member support is not, initial member-SU protection dominates outbound disadvantage, and the same member's SU-minus-IS gap turns negative because investment erodes inward protection while leaving the outbound handicap. A theorem deriving all this from meaningful utility, technology and conduct restrictions would add content. Merely separately solving two witnesses leaves the R18 generality gate open.

No inspected earlier proposition has been proved here logically equivalent to the **entire** joint sequence. This is a precise non-finding, not a priority claim; the three high-risk full-proof gaps block a conclusive negative absorption test.

### Candidate 3 received from derivation team: skeptical assessment

The proposed full-compatible affine formula has `R_l(d) = [-rho d + A d^2/(2 tau^2)]/kappa^2`, with positive `A` and explicit primitive coefficients. If independently proved, its stationary and zero-return thresholds are genuine analytic comparative statics, not a numerical graph. The contrast between homogeneous Cournot and differentiated Bertrand can add useful economic interpretation to the original residual-cost result. At the R16 witness, however, the outsider best-response adoption cutoff lies **below both** the technological ceiling and the formal quadratic minimum. Therefore no observed U-shaped policy response or reentry of SU preference has been established along the endogenous-adoption range. The defensible new implication is the **opposite local comparative directions**, conditional on a maintained strict adoption equilibrium. Turning/reentry expressions outside that range are algebraic boundaries, not economic equilibrium predictions.

However: (a) a national-welfare effect of a foreign cost change is a standard incidence object; (b) the formula is a particular affine market calculation; (c) it does not itself prove initial SU advantage or the investment equilibrium. Therefore this audit classifies it **potentially publishable bounded increment; Level4 not certified**. The separate derivation and independent referee notes now provide an exact adoption best-response cutoff and a global-Nash argument in the fully compatible affine price subgame; these strengthen correctness, not automatically novelty. To survive maximum absorption it needs the exact institutional/adoption implication, an explicit contrast with the closest residual-cost/variable-compatibility theorem, and resolution of missing BFG2026/Maggi2026/DP3816 texts. No numbered theorem from those inaccessible texts is invented.

## 5. Editorial claim control

Allowed: “We derive a bounded primitive comparative-static result for the residual cost of a privately purchased interface, preserving consumer-consistent welfare, and distinguish it from a broader conditional institutional-feedback mechanism.” Only say it is proved once mathematical audit passes.

Not allowed: “first endogenous compatibility”; “first public standards followed by firm adaptation”; “first Cournot/Bertrand compatibility comparison”; “general incompatibility necessarily protects members”; “larger bloc always favors harmonization”; “universal primitive theorem” while reaction envelopes remain assumed; or “priority established” because no identical source was located.

Recommended present decision: preserve R18 as Level2 unless the derivation team closes **all** user Level3 requirements. Keep any residual-cost theorem or counterexample as a separately scoped research addition. The literature evidence presently supports **no certified Level4**. A JICT-style bounded applied-theory manuscript remains a reasonable editorial option, while an IJIO resubmission claim would need a stronger primitive joint theorem and renewed source access. No submission or original-paper merge is proposed by this audit.
