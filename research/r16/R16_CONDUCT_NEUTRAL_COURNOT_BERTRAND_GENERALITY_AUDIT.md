## Executive result

The editorial concern that the private-compatibility-induced reversal of government standardization preferences is **only a three-country linear Cournot calculation** can now be answered with *two structurally different competitive price/quantity environments, both generated by economically coherent consumer choice and fulfilled network expectations*, together with **the very same conduct-neutral sufficient mechanism**.

### Mathematically verified components

1. **G1 strict private-investment selection (R12):** A sufficiently wide gap between the outsider's profit gain from buying a member-standard interface and every member's / separate-standards firm's alternative investment gain yields the unique outsider-only continuation in SU and unique no-investment continuation in SW. It applies to equilibrium operating profits *from any conduct game*, not Cournot FOCs.
2. **G3 integrated national welfare incidence (R12/R13):** Take quasilinear consumer optimization with fulfilled network expectations. Let \(\mathcal I(a)=\sum_i q_iG_i'(a)-q_O p_O'(a)+\sum_{h\in H}(p_h-C_h')q_h'(a)\). If \(\mathcal I'(a)\ge0\) and a finite upper Riemann sum satisfies \(n^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^n \mathcal I(j/n)<-G_0\), where \(G_0>0\) is the original SU-minus-IS government advantage, the representative member's policy preference reverses. This is a **sufficient condition**, not a tautologically asserted post-adoption government choice. The path is a smooth accounting interpolation between *two discrete adoption profiles*, not a new firm action.
3. **Cournot nonvacuity:** Original R9 certified parameter tuple \(v=6/25,c=1/10,d=1/20,F=9/100\) satisfies **both G1 and G3** with exact rational inequalities; original propositions and proofs unchanged. R15 upgrades this from one linear demand to an open, infinite-dimensional \(C^2\)-demand neighborhood and a fully solved nonlinear-demand exemplar. This is still a quantity-choice subfamily.
4. **Bertrand nonvacuity under the SAME economic welfare convention:** For differentiated products, quadratic baseline consumer utility \(U_0(q)=\mathbf1^\top q-\frac12q^\top M_0q\) with \(M_0=(1-\gamma)I+\gamma\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top\), and fulfilled external network benefit \(g=vCq\), consumer optimization yields
   \[
   p=\mathbf1-(M_0-vC)q,\qquad
   CS=U_0+g\cdot q-p\cdot q=\tfrac12q^\top M_0q.
   \]
   Firms commit prices and correctly anticipate the fulfilled network demand \(q=(M_0-vC)^{-1}(\mathbf1-p)\) where interior. This consumer-optimized welfare is **not** the inconsistent prior R10 \(CS=\frac12q^\top(M_0-vC)q\) used when claiming a joint fixed-parameter reversal.
   At the R16 parameters
   \[
   \gamma=23/25,\quad v=33/500,\quad c=2/125,\quad d=1/500,\quad F=1/40,
   \]
   every actual market/game continuation satisfies the stated results, with exact rational proofs:
   \[
   \overline g_H=\overline g_{SW}\simeq0.01515717180
   <F=0.025<g_O^0\simeq0.02981641506.
   \]
   Member-government SU-minus-IS gaps:
   \[
   G_0\simeq+0.00277400733148>0,\qquad
   G_1\simeq-0.000907433889334<0.
   \]
   Pure adoption equilibria SU=outsider-only and SW=none **unique**, including checks against all weak equilibria in 8 SU and 64 SW investment profiles.

**Crucial qualifier:** The original R9 Cournot and newly verified Bertrand example have **different numerical parameters** and different product-differentiation assumptions. The shared theorem is about the same *economic mechanism and sufficient conditions*, not that one common parameter vector is proven to work under both conduct modes. An exploratory random search did not find an identical-parameter joint witness in 260,000 sampled differentiated-model tuples; this is merely negative computational evidence, **not an impossibility theorem**.

## Price game: global best response and exclusion of inactive-product equilibria

The distinction between product-market **interior first-order equations** and genuine Nash equilibrium is crucial when network effects and price competition can cause some brands to be unpurchased.

- For every one of the **19 distinct** market used-standard/marginal-cost configurations encountered in the complete SU/SW/IS adoption stage, \(M=M_0-vC\) is symmetric positive definite by exact Sylvester principal-minor checks. The fulfilled-expectations quantity demand at any price is the **unique complementarity solution**:
  \(q_i\ge0,\ p_i-1+(Mq)_i\ge0,\ q_i[p_i-1+(Mq)_i]=0\).
- Every original candidate equilibrium has all three quantities and margins strictly positive. Its interior price FOC uses the *full equilibrium demand* Jacobian \(-M^{-1}\), including network adjustments. Thus \(q_i=(M^{-1})_{ii}(p_i-c_i)\).
- In each individual market, we enumerated **all four price-deviation active sets containing the deviating firm**, including two-rival exits, one-rival exit and full three-firm trade. On every active-set interval in the deviating price, the firm's operating profit is a concave quadratic. All feasible stationary candidates, finite boundaries, and zero-profit no-sales deviations were evaluated using exact rationals: **211 feasible candidate prices**, with **no profitable deviation**.
- Furthermore, for every proper nonempty active-product set in each of the 19 market configurations, the hypothetical equilibrium prices of the remaining active firms imply **the excluded firm's reservation/choke price exceeds its marginal cost**. The excluded firm has a strict profitable small-price-entry deviation. **171 exact profitable-entry inequalities** exclude all such corner equilibria. All firms inactive cannot be an equilibrium because of the positive reservation value. The all-positive price-game equilibrium is consequently **unique** in each of the 19 environments.
- These are proofs for this stated linear differentiated fulfilled-expectations model; they do **not** imply that every model of network effects possesses a unique price equilibrium.

## Common G3 integrated welfare mechanism verified under *Bertrand*, not merely endpoint signs

Use the artificial compatibility-cost path \(a\in[0,1]\), with member standard group already compatible, outsider cross-group network intensity increasing continuously and outsider market marginal cost decreasing from \(c\) to \(d\). Specifically, the 3x3 inverse-demand coefficient matrix is
\[
M(a)=\begin{pmatrix}
m&b&h\\ b&m&h\\ h&h&o
\end{pmatrix},
\quad m=1-v,\quad b=\gamma-v,\quad h=\gamma-va,\quad o=1-va,
\quad r_O(a)=c+(d-c)a.
\]
For two symmetric home firms with price \(p_h\) and per-firm output \(x\), one outsider output \(y\), let \(A=M(a)^{-1}\); the unique interior differentiated Bertrand FOCs give
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
m+b+1/A_{hh}&h\\
2h&o+1/A_{OO}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1-r_O(a)\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The **consumer-optimized** national member-market welfare is
\[
W(a)=CS(a)+2\,p_h(a)x(a)
=(1+\gamma+2/A_{hh})x^2+2\gamma xy+\tfrac12y^2.
\]
The outsider-destination profit of a home firm is unaffected by outsider-only adoption and cancels from \(W(1)-W(0)\). These symbolic market equations hold for the *same* reference network-externality consumer class used in the R13 general welfare identity.

Define \(I(a)=W'(a)\). **Exact rational symbolic proof** (R16 G3 script) establishes:
- The full \(M(a)\) is positive definite, quantities \(x(a),y(a)>0\), and interior prices \(p_h,p_O>r\) for *every* \(a\in[0,1]\).
- The numerator and denominator polynomials of \(W''(a)\) have **all strictly positive Bernstein-basis coefficients on [0,1]**, of degrees 13 and 16 respectively. Therefore **\(I(a)\) is strictly increasing**, even though it **changes sign** from negative to positive along the path.
- The **exact** right-upper-Riemann certificate for \(n=64\) reads
  \[
  \frac1{64}\sum_{j=1}^{64}I(j/64)
  \simeq-0.00280224531698
  <-G_0\simeq-0.00277400733148.
  \]
  The inequality is checked as an *exact rational comparison*, not on rounded decimals. It guarantees \(G_1<0\) under the **same R12 integrated-incidence theorem** used to account for the original Cournot success case.
- A test of the old original R12 pointwise constant-loss bound is **not** required; for both Cournot and Bertrand, \(I\) changes sign and the integrated G3 criterion is what explains reversal.

Thus **G1 + G3 is a model-conduct-neutral sufficient mechanism with fully verified nonempty Cournot and Bertrand applications**, not simply a rearrangement of government endpoint welfare numbers.

## Function-space portability of Bertrand: a new testable corollary, subject to stated strategic regularity

The Bertrand witness is not another isolated linear-demand knife edge. Restrict to consumer-optimized, twice/three-times continuously differentiable, strictly concave baseline utilities
\[
U_\epsilon(q)=\mathbf1^\top q-\tfrac12q^\top M_0q+\epsilon H(q)
\]
on a compact quantity domain containing all relevant market allocations, with **fulfilled expectations** \(g=vCq\). Consumer optimality then yields \(p=\nabla U_\epsilon(q)+vCq\) and
\[
CS(q)=U_\epsilon(q)-q^\top\nabla U_\epsilon(q),
\]
not an independently invented surplus function. If \(\|\,\epsilon H\,\|_{C^3}\) is sufficiently small, all network-feedback demand matrices remain regular and strongly monotone, preserving unique consumer allocation for any price vector in a relevant bounded price set.

**Existence/persistence claim (conditional on the proven benchmark and economically bounded price actions):** At the exact Bertrand witness above there exists a nonempty \(C^3\)-open neighborhood of \(U_0\) such that every admissible utility in that neighborhood preserves:
- a unique positive, regular price-competition Nash continuation in all finite standard-support market configurations;
- the strict G1 firm adoption selection in SU/SW;
- a strictly positive SU advantage before adoption and a strictly negative advantage afterwards.

**Proof idea needing referee scrutiny:** On a bounded relevant price set, price-game payoffs and their local derivatives depend continuously on \(U_\epsilon\); the exact baseline global profit-deviation and price-equilibrium uniqueness certificate excludes additional equilibria away from the original profile by compactness; the original interior price FOC Jacobian is nonsingular and its own-price Hessians strictly negative, allowing the implicit function theorem and local equilibrium continuity/uniqueness near the baseline. The strict finite adoption and policy signs persist. The global network quantity complementarity remains unique by strong concavity. This is an **existence** theorem, not a constructive explicit lower bound for the radius; a journal proof should spell out the compact truncation and global corner/price-strategy assumptions before submission.

**Numerically fully re-solved independent nonlinear example:** With
\[
U_\epsilon(q)=\mathbf1^\top q-\frac12q^\top M_0q
-\frac{\epsilon}{4}\sum_i q_i^4,\quad \epsilon=10^{-4},
\]
the consumer-derived inverse price satisfies \(p=1-(M_0-vC)q-\epsilon(q_i^3)_i\) and consumer surplus is
\(CS=\frac12q^\top M_0q+\frac{3\epsilon}{4}\sum_i q_i^4\).
All 19 market configurations and every SU/SW/IS adoption profile were re-solved for actual nonlinear-price equilibrium FOCs with the full feedback Jacobian. Verified unique **firm-stage** adoption in this numerical implementation (the numerical test by itself is not a proof of all global *nonlinear* price deviations); SU-minus-IS member welfare gaps:
\[
+0.00272946704741\ \longrightarrow\ -0.000909776672692.
\]
Both are strict with robust positive adoption deviation margins. The nonlinear example supports, but alone does not establish, the general C³ openness argument.
